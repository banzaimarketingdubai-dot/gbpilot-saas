# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Proactive AI-Native Google Maps Growth Copilot & GBP Autopilot SaaS

## 1. Executive Summary & Product Vision
**GBPilot (Autonomous Growth Copilot)** transforms local SEO management from a passive, dashboard-bound task into a proactive, automated 24/7 AI-driven operation. Traditional platforms (BrightLocal, LocalFalcon, Surfer Local) act as passive monitoring tools—forcing business owners or agency managers to log in, analyze complex Geo-Grid heatmaps, decipher competitor changes, and guess what actions to take.

GBPilot acts as an autonomous **Senior Local SEO Growth Manager**:
- **Continuous 24/7 Intelligence:** Scans Google Business Profile (GBP) API signals, daily Geo-Grid SERP rank shifts, competitor movements (photos, reviews, categories, keywords), and incoming customer review sentiment.
- **Proactive Next Best Action Engine:** Delivers up to 5 prioritized, high-impact growth options every morning.
- **1-Click Execution & Full Autopilot Mode:** Every option comes with pre-generated content (LSI-rich posts, branded geotagged images, custom responses, schema fixes) executable in a single click or delegated to 100% background Autopilot.
- **Conversational AI Growth Copilot:** A dedicated `/copilot` chat interface equipped with tool calling (function calling) for natural language insights, custom report generation, competitor teardowns, and instant profile mutations.

## 2.1 The Operational Triad: The 3 Core Modules
To eliminate dashboard fatigue and provide crystal-clear value perception, GBPilot is organized around **3 Global Modules (The Operational Triad)** representing three essential marketing roles:

1. 🧭 **«Разведка» (Scout Radar — Глаза & Стратег):**
   - **Role:** Continuous diagnostic intelligence and territory visibility.
   - **Capabilities:** High-density Geo-Grid rank heatmaps (3x3 to 7x7), Competitor Espionage Radar (tracking rival photo/review/category moves), and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) tracking brand citations inside Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and ChatGPT Search.
2. 🤖 **«Ассистент» (Action Copilot — Руки & Маркетолог):**
   - **Role:** Autonomous execution of high-impact optimizations.
   - **Capabilities:** Proactive 1-Click Action cards, automated weekly localized Google Posts, 15-minute LSI-rich review responses (with neighborhood and entity injection), and peak-traffic ("Open Now") schedule matching.
3. 🛡️ **«Гард» (Defense Shield — Щит & Контрразведка):**
   - **Role:** 24/7 profile integrity defense and anti-sabotage.
   - **Capabilities:** Cryptographic snapshot of canonical business baseline data, real-time monitoring against crowdsourced Google Maps edits, 5-minute automated rollback (Diff Auto-Revert), and FTC-compliant review velocity pacing (Review Speedometer).

## 3. Killer Features & Unfair Advantages

1. **Profile Guard System (Data Integrity Reverter)**
   - Protects against malicious crowdsourced edits. Reverts unauthorized changes to business hours, categories, or phone numbers back to the baseline within 5 minutes.
2. **Dynamic Hours & Peak-Traffic Matching**
   - Correlates search demand peaks with the "Open Now" ranking factor. Proactively recommends extending hours (e.g., staying open 1 hour later on weekends) to capture high-converting traffic.
3. **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & LLM Share-of-Voice**
   - Expands tracking beyond Google Maps to measure brand visibility inside generative AI engines. Automatically targets placement in local "Best of" lists which fuel AI answers.
4. **FTC-Compliant Semantic Review Speedometer**
   - Prevents algorithmic penalties and FTC fines ($53,088 per violation) by managing natural *Review Velocity*. Avoids illegal "Review Gating" while maximizing positive sentiment generation.
5. **Rank Replay GIF Engine (B2B Sales Enablement)**
   - Automatically generates animated GIFs demonstrating a prospect's 30-day "reddening" Geo-Grid rank drop. Used by White-Label agencies to drastically increase cold outreach conversion rates.
6. **Hybrid Dual-Contour Architecture**
   - Uses direct, authenticated Google Business Profile API (up to 300 QPM) for instant 1-Click execution, while using an isolated parser network to scan competitor Geo-Grids without violating Places API Terms of Service.

## 4. Proactive Recommendation Engine Strategy (Next Best Action Matrix)

The Proactive Recommendation Engine runs on a daily schedule (or trigger-based events) per active GBP location. It evaluates parameters across 4 intelligence vectors:
1. **Geo-Grid Delta Vector:** Rank changes across 3x3, 5x5, or 7x7 grid points.
2. **Competitor Movement Vector:** Competitor photo updates, review surge, category changes, business hour extensions.
3. **GBP Health & Profile Integrity Vector:** Missing attributes, missing booking URLs, un-replied reviews, stale post count (>7 days).
4. **Sentiment & Demand Vector:** Review keyword trends, peak engagement hour shifts.

### 5 Core Recommendation Option Types & Payloads

Each daily action recommendation strictly adheres to the following structure:
- **Impact Badge:** `⚡ High Impact` (Rank/Traffic booster), `🚨 Rank Alert` (Drop prevention), `💬 Review Opportunity` (Reputation/CTR), `🛠️ Profile Fix` (Conversion rate optimization), `🌙 Demand Spike` (Temporal optimization).
- **Problem / Opportunity Statement:** Clear human explanation of the trigger.
- **Target Result Projection:** e.g., `+18% Maps Impressions`, `Recover #2 Rank in Sector B`, `+15% Direct Conversions`.
- **1-Click Action Payload:** Pre-computed code/content ready to execute instantly via Google Business Profile API or AI worker pipeline.

#### Example Action Scenarios:
1. **⚡ Competitor Photo Counter-Strategy**
   - *Problem:* Competitor "Artisan Bakery" uploaded 12 new high-res photos this week and gained top 3 grid placement.
   - *Action:* Generate 3 AI-enhanced branded photos with LSI geotag metadata.
   - *CTA:* `[Execute in 1-Click]`
2. **🚨 Geo-Grid Rank Drop Alert**
   - *Problem:* Position dropped from #2 to #5 for keyword `"breakfast cafe"` at 3 grid points (North Sector B).
   - *Action:* Publish targeted Google Post featuring LSI phrases `"fresh organic breakfast"`, `"Sector B espresso cafe"`, with custom CTA button.
   - *CTA:* `[Approve & Publish Post]`
3. **💬 Review Sentiment Surge**
   - *Problem:* 4 new 5-star reviews received containing un-replied text highlighting "dog-friendly patio".
   - *Action:* Send LSI-rich responses reinforcing `"best dog-friendly patio cafe in downtown"`.
   - *CTA:* `[Send 4 Responses]`
4. **🛠️ Conversion Funnel Optimization**
   - *Problem:* Missing "Online Reservation / Table Booking" action link detected on GBP profile while competitors have active links.
   - *Action:* Inject reservation URL `https://booking.example.com/table` to boost conversion by estimated +15%.
   - *CTA:* `[Fix Now]`
5. **🌙 Demand Pattern Shift**
   - *Problem:* 6 reviews in past 14 days mention late night dessert demand; competitor opens till 11 PM on weekends.
   - *Action:* Extend Friday & Saturday closing hours by 1 hour on GBP profile schedule.
   - *CTA:* `[Update GBP Schedule]`

## 4. Zero-Friction Onboarding & Global Customer Journey Maps (CJM)

The platform is designed around a Product-Led Growth (PLG) model with **Zero-Friction Onboarding**. The time-to-value is less than 60 seconds, providing immediate insights before requiring credit card details or complex setups.

### SCENARIO A: Business Has NO Existing Google Business Profile
*Target:* New businesses, expanding locations, or legacy offline businesses missing from Google Maps.

#### Detailed 4-Step Customer Path:
1. **Step 1: AI Website & Domain Analysis Engine**
   - User enters their existing business website URL (or social media page).
   - GBPilot's AI Web Scraper automatically parses meta tags, schema markup, physical address, phone numbers, core services, opening hours, brand mission, and target keywords.
   - Generates a **Pre-Filled Business Entity Draft**.
2. **Step 2: Interactive AI Assistant Interview (The GBP Profiler Assistant)**
   - The AI Copilot (`/copilot`) initiates a guided 2-minute chat interview to collect high-ranking attributes that websites often omit.
3. **Step 3: Geocoding & Geo-Grid Setup**
   - Geocodes physical address into precise latitude/longitude coordinates and prepares Google Business Profile Verification Payload.
4. **Step 4: AI Launchpad & First 5 Proactive Actions**
   - Immediately generates the business's first 5 launch actions (e.g., initial geotagged photos, launch post, and booking URL injection).

---

### SCENARIO B: Business ALREADY HAS an Active Google Business Profile
*Target:* Established local businesses seeking higher rankings, agency clients, or stores suffering from rank drops.

#### Detailed 4-Step "Zero-Friction" Path:
1. **Step 1: Magic Search Scan (No Auth Required)**
   - On the homepage, the user enters their business name or address.
   - Without registration, the system uses public APIs to instantly generate a 3x3 Geo-Grid heatmap and a Profile Completeness Score, highlighting "red zones" (lost traffic) to trigger FOMO.
2. **Step 2: 1-Click Google OAuth Sync**
   - User clicks `[Fix in 1-Click]` and authenticates via Google OAuth.
   - The system instantly imports all profile details, reviews, and posts without manual data entry.
3. **Step 3: AI-Autopilot "Smart Instant Fix" (Quick Wins)**
   - The AI agent instantly generates "Quick Wins": adds missing categories, drafts a welcome Google Post with an offer, and generates LSI-rich responses to the last unreplied reviews.
   - The user clicks a single `[Apply AI Optimization]` button to execute all fixes.
4. **Step 4: 1-Click Local Website Builder**
   - For businesses with poor or missing websites, the system compiles the GBP data into a fully functional, SEO-optimized local landing page with LocalBusiness Schema markup in 2 seconds.

## 7. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & Local LSI Strategy
All AI-generated text, review responses, and Google Posts produced by GBPilot adhere to **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** principles:
- **Local LSI Keywords:** Integrates neighborhood names, nearby landmarks, cross-streets, zip codes, and semantic entity terms (e.g., *"best espresso bar near Central Park"*, *"artisan roaster near 5th Ave"*).
- **Structured Schema Alignment:** Ensures text formats match how Google's Search Experience and Local Pack algorithms index GBP posts and review keywords.
- **Natural Tone & Diversity:** Prevents repetitive patterns by varying tone options (Official & Professional, Warm & Friendly, SEO-Rich & Direct).
